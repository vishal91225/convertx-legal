4. Internet Usage & External Services

ConvertX connects to the internet for the following specific purposes only:

4.1 Currency Exchange Rates

The app fetches live exchange rates from open.er-api.com (a free, public API). This connection only happens when:

  • You open the Currency converter
  • More than 24 hours have passed since the last update
  • You manually tap "Refresh Rates"

4.2 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display ads. Google may collect certain data for ad personalization. Please review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (resettable in Android Settings)
  • IP address (anonymized)
  • Device type and OS version
  • App interaction events

4.3 Google Fonts

The app loads fonts (Outfit, Nunito) from Google Fonts API on first launch. No personal data is sent.

📶 Note: All 500+ unit conversions (except currency) work completely offline. Currency conversion uses cached rates when offline.
🔐

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Android's standard mechanisms:

  • Room Database: Conversion history, favorites, cached currency rates — stored in encrypted local database
  • DataStore: App preferences — stored in Android DataStore (Preferences)
  • All local data is protected by Android's standard app sandboxing
  • Your data is not backed up to cloud services by default

We implement industry-standard security practices, but no system is completely infallible. We recommend keeping your device updated.

🧒

6. Children's Privacy

ConvertX is rated Everyone (All ages) on Google Play. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Since we don't collect personal information from any users, there are no special concerns for children's data. The app is safe for educational use in schools.

⚖️

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Clear History: Settings → Data → Clear History
  • Clear Currency Cache: Settings → Data → Clear Currency Cache
  • Export Data: Settings → Data → Export History as CSV
  • Delete App: Uninstalling ConvertX removes ALL app data from your device
  • Reset Ad ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID
